# The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head

One original funny joke about office life, exactly one line, at most 200 characters.

Entry: `Our team standup is the only meeting where fifteen people spend thirty minutes explaining why the thing from yesterday is still the thing for today.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`).
